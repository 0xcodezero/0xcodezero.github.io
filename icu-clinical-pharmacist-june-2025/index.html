ICU / Clinical Pharmacist - AI Project

Project Overview

An advanced AI-powered clinical decision support system designed to provide a comprehensive analysis of ICU patients' medications and identify potential drug-related problems (DRPs).

AI Technology

This project integrates multiple state-of-the-art AI models to provide comprehensive medication analysis:

  • OpenAI (gpt-4.1-mini)
  • Google Gemini (gemini-2.5-pro)
  • Anthropic Claude (sonnet-4)

Clinical Applications

The system supports ICU clinical pharmacists and physicians by:

  • Identifying potential Drug Related Problems (DRPs)
  • Providing evidence-based medication recommendations
  • Analyzing patient-specific data for personalized care

Data Integration

The system integrates comprehensive patient data from Electronic Medical Records (EMR) using Problem-Oriented Medical Record Identifiers (PomrId) to access:

Medication Profiles

Complete medication history and current regimens

Laboratory Results

Comprehensive lab data for medication monitoring

Progress Notes

Latest ICU team assessments and care plans
